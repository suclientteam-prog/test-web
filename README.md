# Little Star English – NEMO 2 (28/9 – 02/10)

Static website for kids, built from the class "Báo bài" PDF.
Open `index.html` in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Samsung Internet). No server needed.

## Games for 3-year-olds (Play & listen) – no wrong answers, big targets
- Tap & hear (Chạm và nghe): every picture says its word.
- Bubble pop (Bắn bong bóng): pop floating bubbles, hear the word.
- Peekaboo (Ú òa): open gift boxes – "This is a book."
- Shadow match (Tìm cái bóng): pick the matching shadow (2 choices).
- Find the same (Lật thẻ): memory cards, 2 then 3 pairs.
- Pack my bag (Xếp ba lô): tap school things into the backpack – "I have a pencil."

## Lesson activities for 4+ (Learn & practise)
Words, Letter S (with tracing), Listen & find, Ask & answer, Tidy up.

## Rewards
- Stars for every success; every 5 stars unlocks a sticker (16 stickers).
- Sticker book: tap the book button in the top bar.
- Confetti celebration at the end of every game.
- Progress is saved in the browser (localStorage).

## Sound
- Voice: 58 mp3 files in `assets/audio/` (Piper TTS, en-US).
- Background music: `assets/audio/bg_music.mp3` – an original loop made for this site.
  Toggle with the music button; it gets quieter while the voice speaks.
- Sound effects are generated live in the browser (no files).

## Files
- `js/assets.js` – list of media files
- `js/core.js` – lesson words, sound, music, stars, stickers, celebration
- `js/app.js` – pages and games
- `css/style.css` – styles (phones, tablets, laptops, portrait and landscape)
